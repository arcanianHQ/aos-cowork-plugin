# Lumen Audio — Positioning Brief (draft)

**Date:** 2026-05-04
**Author:** Founder (Mara Ellison)
**Status:** raw — not yet reviewed

## Who we are
Lumen Audio is a direct-to-consumer brand making a single product: the Lumen One,
a wired-first open-back headphone aimed at people who work in audio (editors,
mixers, podcast producers) but can't justify a EUR 1,500 studio reference can.
Price point EUR 380. We ship from a small facility outside Porto.

## The problem we think we solve
Prosumers are stuck between EUR 120 "creator" headphones that lie about their
frequency response and EUR 1,500 reference cans they can't afford or justify.
We sit in the EUR 350–450 gap with an honest, measured, repairable product.

## What we believe about the market
- The buyer trusts measurements, not adjectives. Marketing copy that says "rich,
  warm sound" actively repels them.
- They discover gear through YouTube reviewers and two specific forums, not ads.
- They buy once and keep it for years — so LTV is mostly referral, not repeat.
