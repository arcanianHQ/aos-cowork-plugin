# Campaign index — <client>

Every campaign and theme for this client. A **theme** groups campaigns under one
narrative; a **campaign** is one time-boxed effort with its own brief. Built by
`aos-plan-campaign`. The full record of each campaign — the brief, budget,
platforms, KPI — is its per-campaign file, `campaigns/<slug>.md`.

This is the **campaign zone** (schema version 4) — it supersedes the old flat
`dictionaries/campaign.yaml`. It is the filesystem form: a customer without the
`aos-data-layer` overlay holds the full campaign model here.
