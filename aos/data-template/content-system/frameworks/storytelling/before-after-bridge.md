# Before — After — Bridge

> **What it is.** A 10-beat narrative arc built on the classic Before / After /
> Bridge persuasion structure, expanded into a full series. Run once, it yields
> **10 distinct content pieces** — one series — that move a prospect from a vivid
> picture of their current pain, through a vivid picture of the resolved future,
> to the concrete bridge the brand provides between the two.

## Arc

Three acts, not one. Most BAB writing compresses Before/After/Bridge into a single
piece; as a *series* each act gets room to breathe:

- **Act I — Before (beats 1–4):** make the current state undeniable. Not "you have
  a problem" stated once, but the problem seen from four angles until the prospect
  can no longer un-see it.
- **Act II — After (beats 5–7):** make the resolved future concrete and desirable —
  specific, earned, free of hype.
- **Act III — Bridge (beats 8–10):** the brand's path from Before to After. This is
  where the offer appears, and only here — after Before and After have done the work.

The arc persuades because desire is built *before* the offer is named. A prospect
who reaches beat 8 already wants the After; the Bridge just shows the way.

## Beats — the 10-piece series

Each beat is one content piece. Default content type is `linkedin-post`.

| # | Beat | Act | Narrative job | Suggested type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Familiar Pain** | Before | Name the current state in the prospect's own words. | linkedin-post |
| 2 | **The Hidden Cost** | Before | What the status quo quietly costs — time, money, standing. | linkedin-post |
| 3 | **The Failed Fix** | Before | The workarounds the prospect already tried, and why they didn't hold. | linkedin-post |
| 4 | **The Tipping Point** | Before | The moment "good enough" stops being good enough. | linkedin-post |
| 5 | **The Resolved Day** | After | A concrete day-in-the-life once the problem is gone. | linkedin-post |
| 6 | **The Second-Order Win** | After | The bigger gain unlocked beyond the obvious one. | blog-post |
| 7 | **The Proof It's Real** | After | Evidence the After is achievable — a real result, not a promise. | blog-post |
| 8 | **The Bridge Revealed** | Bridge | The brand's path from Before to After — named for the first time. | linkedin-post |
| 9 | **The First Step** | Bridge | The smallest, lowest-risk way to start crossing. | email |
| 10 | **The Open Invitation** | Bridge | A soft, confident call to begin. | linkedin-post |

## Platform note

Canonical run is single-platform (10 LinkedIn posts). The table re-types the two
proof-heavy After beats (6, 7) to `blog-post` and the First Step beat (9) to `email`.
A series **may span multiple content types** — pick per beat.

## When to use

- The prospect's pain is real but **under-felt** — they have normalised it. Act I's
  four Before beats exist precisely to de-normalise it.
- The After is genuinely desirable and the brand can prove it (beat 7).
- A shorter series than Hero's Journey is wanted — 10 beats, three clean acts.

## When NOT to use

- The prospect is already pain-aware and offer-ready — skip to a single piece.
- No credible proof for beat 7 — Act II collapses, the Bridge has nothing to span to.
- The brand cannot articulate a *distinct* Bridge — if the path is generic, the
  series reads as a category explainer, not a reason to choose this brand.
