# Structure — Hook → Insight → CTA (LinkedIn post)

> The general-purpose LinkedIn-post skeleton. Four sections. Fits any beat whose
> job is to **make a point** rather than tell a story.

## Sections

| # | Section | Job | Length hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | Stop the scroll. One claim, question, or tension — must land in the first ~3 lines (above the "see more" fold). | 1–2 short lines |
| 2 | **Context** | Ground the hook. Why this is true / why it matters / who it's for. | 2–4 short paragraphs |
| 3 | **Insight** | The single idea the post exists to deliver. Specific, contestable, useful on its own. | 2–4 short paragraphs |
| 4 | **CTA** | One next move. In a series this is usually soft — a thought to sit with, or a pointer to the next beat. Hard asks (book a call) only on late-arc beats. | 1–2 lines |

## Fit

- The default for most beats of a single-platform series.
- Hero's Journey beats 2, 4, 5, 8, 11. Before-After-Bridge beats 2, 3, 4, 8, 10.
- Any beat whose narrative job in the framework file reads as "name / argue /
  reveal" rather than "tell a moment".

## Anti-patterns

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| Bury the hook under a warm-up sentence | First line *is* the hook |
| Two or three ideas in one post | One insight per post — split the rest into other beats |
| A hard CTA on an early-arc beat | Match CTA strength to where the beat sits in the arc |
| In-body link | Link in the first comment (LinkedIn reach rule) |
| A hook that the insight doesn't pay off | The insight must deliver exactly what the hook promised |
