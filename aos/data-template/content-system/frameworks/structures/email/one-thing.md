# Structure — One Thing (email)

> The single-purpose email skeleton. Five short sections. The whole email serves
> **one idea and one ask** — nothing competes with it.

## Sections

| # | Section | Job | Length hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Subject** | The hook. Curiosity or value. The reason the email gets opened. | 30–55 chars |
| 2 | **Context** | One line that sets up why this email, now. No throat-clearing. | 1–2 lines |
| 3 | **The one thing** | The single idea / offer / next step. The entire reason the email exists. | 2–4 short paragraphs |
| 4 | **CTA** | One ask, one link. A single bolded line is fine. | 1 line |
| 5 | **Sign-off** | Warm, human, one-to-one. A real name. | 1 line |

## Fit

- The nurture / first-step beats of a series — the ones routed to `email`.
- Hero's Journey beat 9 (The Road Back — integration).
- Before-After-Bridge beat 9 (The First Step).
- Any beat where a warm audience is asked to *do* one concrete thing.

## Anti-patterns

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| Two CTAs / two links | One ask — move the rest to another beat |
| A subject line that over-promises | Subject matches exactly what the email delivers |
| A long warm-up before the point | Context is one line; the one thing comes fast |
| Heavy template / image-heavy layout | Plain-text-feeling email — it reads as personal |
| Corporate sign-off | A real person signs it |
