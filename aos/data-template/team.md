## How to use

- **Natural language** — "Help me figure out positioning" → routed to Iris automatically.
- **`@Name` shorthand** — `@Marcus give me this week's plan` → goes straight to Marcus's anchor skill (`aos-plan`) with active client context.
- **Convene a meeting** (AOS-1227, coming soon) — `@team debate our positioning` → 3-round multi-colleague debate.

## What's outside the team

The team covers the *thinking and drafting* half of marketing + sales. A
few execution surfaces stay with the operator and external tools:

- Live ad campaign push — Hunter discovers, Anna analyses, Marcus prioritises; the operator clicks "launch" in Google Ads / Meta Editor.
- Email send — Quinn drafts; Postmark / ActiveCampaign sends.
- Phone / Zoom calls — Vera drafts brief + summary; a human takes the call.
- Visual design / creative production — Iris drafts the brief; designer / Figma / AI image tool produces.
- Live community engagement — Echo drafts; a human replies, reacts, moderates.
