# build-brand — Brand strategy analysis template

Paste into deliverables when running `/build-brand`. Adapt section titles to the engagement’s voice; keep the logic.

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    BRAND STRATEGY ANALYSIS
                  (100M Branding Framework)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BRAND: [Business/Personal name]
DATE: [Analysis date]

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                  STEP 1: IDEAL CUSTOMER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TARGET AVATAR: [Specific description]

CRITERIA CHECK:
□ Growing Market: [Yes/No - explanation]
□ Has Money: [Yes/No - explanation]
□ Easy to Target: [Yes/No - where they congregate]
□ In Pain: [Yes/No - specific pain points]

AVATAR SCORE: [0-4] criteria met

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                  STEP 2: WHAT THEY LIKE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OUTCOMES THEY WANT:
• [Outcome 1]
• [Outcome 2]
• [Outcome 3]

PROBLEMS THEY HATE:
• [Problem 1]
• [Problem 2]
• [Problem 3]

PEOPLE THEY ADMIRE:
• [Person/Brand 1]
• [Person/Brand 2]

CONTENT THEY CONSUME:
• [Type/Platform 1]
• [Type/Platform 2]

VALUES THEY HOLD:
• [Value 1]
• [Value 2]

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                  STEP 3: CURRENT ASSOCIATIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

POSITIVE ASSOCIATIONS (keep/amplify):
• [Association 1] → Aligned with: [what they like]
• [Association 2] → Aligned with: [what they like]

NEGATIVE/NEUTRAL ASSOCIATIONS (remove/minimize):
• [Association 1] → Problem: [why it's hurting]
• [Association 2] → Problem: [why it's hurting]

MISSING ASSOCIATIONS (opportunities):
• [What they like that you're NOT associated with]
• [People/values/outcomes you could pair with]

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                  STEP 4: OPTIMIZATION STRATEGY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CURRENT POSITION: [Where brand sits now]

RECOMMENDED DIRECTION:
□ Up Market - because: [reason]
□ Down Market - because: [reason]
□ Adjacent - because: [reason]
□ Broader - because: [reason]
□ Narrower - because: [reason]

PIVOT ANALYSIS:
• Will LOSE: [who/what]
• Will GAIN: [who/what]
• NET ASSESSMENT: [Positive/Negative - worth it?]

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                  BRAND HEALTH METRICS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REACH: [Low/Medium/High] - [evidence]
INFLUENCE: [Low/Medium/High] - [evidence]
DIRECTION: [Towards/Away/Neutral] - [evidence]

OVERALL BRAND STRENGTH: [Weak/Developing/Strong]

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                  ACTION PLAN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

IMMEDIATE (This Week):
□ [Action 1]
□ [Action 2]

SHORT-TERM (This Month):
□ [Action 1]
□ [Action 2]

ONGOING (Build Over Time):
□ [Action 1]
□ [Action 2]

ASSOCIATIONS TO CREATE:
• Content: [specific content to make]
• People: [collaborations to pursue]
• Products: [offerings to develop]
• Values: [stands to take]

ASSOCIATIONS TO REMOVE:
• [What to stop doing/showing]

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                  BELIEF CHECK
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BELIEFS AFFECTING BRAND DECISIONS:
• [Belief that may cause weak positioning]
• [Belief that may cause wrong associations]

(Trace the belief if the customer resists charging premium,
being visible, or making strong associations — what would someone
have to believe to stay invisible?)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

End with: **What did we get wrong? What's missing?**
