# Intake method — classifying imports and running the fresh-discovery intake

Companion to `aos-discovery-package/SKILL.md`. The import-classification map,
the discovery checklist, and the fresh-discovery intake question set.

---

## §1 — Import-classification map

Each imported piece is filed into a typed `inbox/` subfolder. Classify by what
the document **is**, not what it is named.

| `inbox/` subfolder | What lands here |
|---|---|
| `strategy/` | Strategy decks, positioning briefs, business plans, brand guidelines, pitch documents |
| `transcripts/` | Call / meeting transcripts, interview notes, recorded conversations |
| `correspondence/` | Emails, DMs, founder notes, client messages |
| `research/` | Market research, competitor docs, analytics exports, survey results, customer research |
| `brand-material/` | Existing copy, taglines, published content, the website export, visual-identity notes |
| `inbox/` root (flagged) | Anything whose type is genuinely unclear — filed, flagged, not force-fitted |

Each imported file gets a one-line provenance note (a sidecar or a frontmatter
line): **where it came from** and **its date**. The content itself is never
altered.

---

## §2 — The discovery checklist

Measure the imported material against this checklist — it is the signal
`aos-build-brand-system` will need for the 9-file profile. Each row is **covered**
(the import answers it) or a **gap** (the intake must capture it).

| Area | Covered when the import contains… |
|---|---|
| The offer | what the client sells, pricing, the guarantee / risk-reversal |
| The customer / ICP | who buys, why, the job they hire the product for, testimonials |
| The founder / decision-makers | who decides, named, with their stated beliefs / fears |
| Positioning | the category, the competitors, the "only one who…" |
| Channels | where the client reaches the market today, what works |
| Competitors | named competitors, not just "the market" |
| Results to date | analytics, conversion, what has been tried and what happened |
| Voice | real published copy — "voice in the wild", not a description of voice |

---

## §3 — The fresh-discovery intake

For each **gap**, run that area's intake — focused, in rounds, conversational.
Write the answers into `inbox/discovery/<area>.md` so they harvest like any
other source. Do not run an area the import already covers.

### Offer
- What exactly do you sell? One product or several?
- Price points, and how you arrived at them?
- Warranty / return / guarantee — what do you promise, and what do you not?

### Customer / ICP
- Who is your best customer — the one you wish you had more of?
- What job are they hiring you to do — in their words?
- Who is explicitly *not* a good fit?

### Founder / decision-makers
- Who makes the marketing calls? (name them)
- What does each believe a good customer / good marketing looks like?
- What worries them about the business or the market?

### Channels
- Where do customers actually find you today?
- Which channel drives sales vs. just assists — and how do you know?
- What have you tried that did not work?

### Competitors
- Who do you watch? Who do you lose deals to?
- Where is each of them better than you — honestly?

### Results
- What are your current numbers — traffic, conversion, revenue trend?
- What has been tried in the last year, and what happened?

---

## §4 — The readiness check

After import + intake, score each of the 9 brand-profile files: is there enough
in `inbox/` to draft it (**green**), thin-but-draftable (**amber**), or no signal
(**red**)? The summary states the overall verdict — **ready for the brand build**,
or **another intake round needed** — and names every red / amber file's gap.
An honest amber/red is worth more than a green that papers over a thin import.
